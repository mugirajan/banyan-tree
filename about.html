FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer monthly billing for corporate accounts?

Yes. Corporate clients receive a single consolidated invoice each month, with trips broken down by employee, department, or cost centre so you can reconcile them easily.

How quickly can you arrange a vehicle?

Scheduled bookings are confirmed within the hour. For urgent requests in the cities we operate in, we can usually have a car with you in 45 to 60 minutes.

Are your drivers background-checked?

Every driver is police-verified and licence-checked before joining us, and we run regular refresher training on safety, route knowledge, and client etiquette.

Can we track our vehicles during a trip?

Yes. Every vehicle carries GPS, and corporate clients get live tracking along with trip and usage reports through our online dashboard.

Which cities do you operate in?

We cover ten cities across India, with offices in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, and Pune. Outstation trips can start from any of these locations.
