Monthly Car Rental: Is It Right for You?

Home Car Rental Guide

Monthly car rental means you keep the same car for one month or more, and you pay one fixed amount. Daily car rental only with Driver. We are not providing cars without driver. Many people ask us if this is a good idea. The answer depends on how you travel. Let us keep it simple.

Who Should Take a Monthly Rental?

Monthly rental works well if you travel every day and your route is long. It also helps if you are new in a city, if your own car is in the workshop, or if your office gives you a car allowance. If you drive only on weekends, a daily booking may cost less.

What Does the Price Cover?

A monthly plan usually covers the car, insurance, service, and normal repairs. Fuel and toll are often extra. Driver charge depends on the plan you choose. Always ask for the kilometre limit and the price for extra kilometres. Once you know these three numbers, you can compare any offer.

Monthly Rental Is a Good Fit When

  • You travel almost every day
  • You want one fixed cost each month
  • You do not want service and repair work
  • You are in the city for a few months only
  • You need a car for a project or a season
  • You want the option to change the car later

Rent or Buy?

When you buy a car, you pay a large amount at the start, and the value goes down every year. You also handle insurance, service, and repair. With monthly rental, you pay as you use. If your plan may change in a year, rental is the safer choice.

Buying a car is a long promise. Monthly rental is a short one, and you can change it when your life changes.

Questions to Ask Before You Sign

Ask about the kilometre limit, the extra kilometre rate, the security deposit, the notice period, and what happens if the car needs repair. Ask if you get a replacement car on those days. A good company will answer all of this in writing.
